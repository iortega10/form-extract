# 0.6.0 kind confusion on the lines contract: what to do after S1 failed

Status: debate outcome, 2026-10-08. Participants: reviewer (Claude) and hearth's model, three
rounds through the real `hearth` CLI, discussion only (no mutating call fired, no provider call
made, no code written). Nothing here is built. The owner reviews before any turn is cut.
Related: `0.6.0-line-contract-debate.md` (section 4 for kind derivation vs statement, section 12 for
the format of the change-of-mind record), `0.6.0-style-cue-spec.md` (S1), `0.6.0-gold-v4-matrix-spec.md`.

## 0. Goal and headline

Goal: decide what to try next against `text>single` kind confusion (gemini-3.5-flash-lite, lines
contract, `fix2`, 16 dev tabs, 6 s paced), after S1 (`fix2_style`, shading cue) made it worse.

Agreed direction, in one paragraph. Do not spend a live call first. The single cheapest and most
informative step is an **offline replay** of an existing `--record-out` dump: read the per-tag numbers,
histogram the kind confusions by option cardinality, and apply a deterministic resolver rule
("a `single` with exactly one option element is not a single choice") to the dump. If that recovers
most of the `text>single` bucket, the lever is a **versioned resolver-side kind derivation from
cardinality plus marker evidence**, and further prompt wording is moot. If it recovers little, the
failure is attention and the next step is one live A/B of `fix2` against `fix2` plus a
recurrence-only tag. Styling, cue-word tags and length thresholds are off the table. The reviewer's
original premise about the gold's style layer was wrong (section 2) and that changes the reading of S1.

## 2. The premise that was wrong (reviewer)

The reviewer's brief said "options are bold AND filled, typed answers plain". The generator says
otherwise: the only styled cell in the whole dev and held-out set is the selected option of a
no-glyph row, and bold is only ever set together with fill, so a bold flag would add zero bits over
`Element.fill`. Consequences:

- S1 did not fail because "shading means different things in different layouts" in the abstract. It
  failed because its sentence said shaded implies option, and the only cells it fires on are selected
  options in `no_glyph_answer` rows. The model concluded the unshaded cells were the answers, which is
  the 9/9 -> 1/9 collapse. `text_field` 1 -> 2 of 12 is noise: no text row changed.
- So S1 is evidence against that sentence on this gold, not against styling as a lever. The reviewer's
  reason (1) is restated: **this synthetic gold has no usable option/answer style signal, and the
  one it has is inverted (selected)**. Whether real forms shade inputs or options remains unmeasured.
- The scorer never reads style, so this gold cannot reward a style cue even in principle.

## 4. The proposed rule (narrow, cardinality plus marker, no text)

For a field the model stated as `single` whose option-element set has **cardinality exactly 1**:

- a lone marker glyph in the field's band (knowledge the resolver already has: `MarkerClassification`,
  `decision.marker_element_id`) -> re-derive as `bool` (the `canned.py:64` precedent);
- no marker anywhere in the field's band -> re-derive as `text`.

Properties and guards, as agreed:

- It reads **no text**. No recurrence clause, no pool vocabulary, no cue words. The recurrence clause
  hearth first proposed was dropped in round 3 as unnecessary: a one-option single-select does not
  exist as a real shape.
- It must **not touch `multi`**: a one-option `multi` is a real shape (one checkbox in a "check any
  that apply" block).
- It cannot touch `bool>single` or `text>bool`. The sheet does not separate those (typed `Recorded`
  versus an unselected `Recorded | Waived` choice), and the plan accepts that limit.
- Sparing the cases that must not regress: `yes_no_row` cites 2 options, `side_by_side` cites 2,
  `no_glyph_answer` cites at least 2 (its failure is selection, not kind), matrix cites a shared header
  id. The size guard spares all of them. The builder must still assert this on the dump, not trust it.
- Known bounded error: a genuine degenerate lone choice cell beside a blank "Other: ____" line, with
  no glyph, becomes `text`. From the sheet that is indistinguishable from a value cell; the error is
  rare and the defensible reading.
- The model's stated `control_type` is kept in provenance and the override decision is recorded.
  Cached records move, so this is a resolver version change.

## 6. Decision rule for promoting anything

Denominator: the count of `text>single` confusions, and that count's share of all confusions. Both
are one draw of a noisy run, so state them together.

| offline result of experiment 2 | action |
|---|---|
| recovers at least 70% of `text>single` AND `text>single` is at least 40-50% of all confusions | spend the one held-out run on the rule; ship if no regression on `yes_no_row`, `no_glyph_answer`, `side_by_side`, `matrix` |
| recovers 50-70% | ship the pure cardinality plus marker rule only; do not add a recurrence net to chase the last few |
| recovers under 50%, or the bucket is under about 20% of confusions | the rule is a toy (about 5 matched at most, inside noise); run experiment 4 |

The 70% gate is a **screening** gate for spending the held-out run, not the ship gate. Ship order:
offline histogram, offline override re-score, one held-out run on a frozen implementation with the
override on, ship only if nothing regressed. Versioned and default-flippable like any other contract
change. The reviewer and hearth both agreed that thresholds were judgment, not derived; the owner may
move them.

## 7. `label_two_rows`

0/20 under every variant. The S1 spec asserted it was a kind failure ("reads a typed answer beside a
label as an option") without checking. The gold identity is the two stacked labels as one set, so a
model that emits two fields or one line citing only the first label never reaches the kind comparison:
a grouping miss. Hearth's bet is grouping; the reviewer accepts it as a bet, not a fact. The per-tag
`matched_ignoring_kind` in experiment 1 settles it: near 0 means grouping (the owner is the row
lattice / merge logic where R1 fixed `merged_tall`, or the gold), near 20 means it is a `text>single`
bucket and kind owns it. The v4 matrix spec already keeps it out of prompt-tuning decisions.

## 8. What must NOT be done

- Do not tune on, or feed back, the held-out set. It runs once on a frozen implementation.
- Do not promote any prompt variant or the override on one dev run, or on re-scored dev dumps alone.
- Do not add a cue-word tag, a length/token threshold, or any tag that reads the gold's label vocabulary.
- Do not widen scoring identity (adding `answer`-role cells to `label_cells + option_cells` or to
  `tab_label_option`). That turns a `text>single` confusion into an agreement and looks like a win.
  It is laundering and sits beside "do not fix gold labels to match".
- Do not edit gold labels or hand-typed literals to make a number move (CLAUDE.md rule 1).
- Do not derive `single` vs `multi`, and do not let the cardinality rule touch `multi`.
- Do not change the json contract's behaviour or the bytes of `base`, `fix1`, `fix2`, `fix2_notags`,
  `fix2_style`; the existing v2/v3 gold hashes and tab-sha tests stay untouched.
- Do not ship an override without recording the model's stated kind and a version bump (cached
  records move).
- Do not re-attempt styling until a gold with a real style layer exists.

  property, not a layout-ambiguity argument.
- Dropped the cue-word tag (b) and the neighbour-length cue after hearth showed the gold writes cue
  words into labels deliberately, so the tag mirrors the generator.
- Accepted that (a) wording has already had its best shot (the worked example and the `?` sentence
  are in `fix2`) and ranks low.
- Accepted offline replay as step 1 instead of any live call, and (iv) that `label_two_rows` may not
  be a kind problem at all.
- Accepted that (d) is orthogonal and must not gate the kind work.
- Accepted hearth's additional "do not" items (identity laundering, version bump for the override,
  no ship on dev-only dumps).

Hearth moved:
- Dropped the recurrence clause of the demotion rule when the reviewer showed that `Recorded` and
  `Recorded | Waived` come from the same pool and cannot be separated by text; the rule became
  cardinality plus marker.
- Conceded the rule cannot reach `bool>single` and `text>bool`.
- Accepted that the denominator for the thresholds is the `text>single` count and its share, not
  `matched` and its +-10.
- Narrowed "derive kind" to `text` and lone-marker `bool`, leaving `single`/`multi` to the model.

Hearth was wrong or loose, caught by the reviewer: (i) its round-1 sentence that the cheapest
experiment needs no calls assumed the dumps exist; whether a baseline `--record-out` was kept is
unknown to both sides; (ii) the inverted gate wording on experiment 4 (section 5 note); (iii) its claim
that `label_two_rows` is "pure grouping" was a bet stated with more confidence than the code reading
supports, and it is not verified until the per-tag number is read; (iv) it called (c) "half-invented
here" because of the canned shape, which is a fixture, not evidence that the resolver derives bool
from cardinality today.

Reviewer was wrong: the style-layer premise, which two prior documents (the S1 spec and the
reviewer's brief) had carried unchecked. The S1 spec also asserted the `label_two_rows` failure mode
without reading its gold identity.
